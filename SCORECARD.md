# The claimed finite-time blow-up of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations with a smooth force, announced by OpenAI on 8 September 2026 with a Lean development

On 8 September 2026 OpenAI announced that an internal model had constructed, for every positive viscosity, a smooth force compactly supported in space and time and a solution of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations starting from rest whose velocity becomes unbounded in finite time while its kinetic energy stays bounded, on space and on the torus. In the Clay formulation this is alternatives (C) and (D), which allow a force; alternatives (A) and (B), the unforced existence question, are untouched, and the day before the announcement Tristan Buckmaster had posted that he and Levent Alpöge had forced blow-up for the Euler equations by the same program, which they credit to Córdoba and Martínez-Zoroa. A Lean development accompanies the paper. A third party reports building it in October: no sorry, the three standard axioms, and statements identical to DeepMind's formal-conjectures file, which has since marked (C) and (D) as solved. A preprint by Bastounis, Circelli and Hansen reports that one lemma's Lean translation is weaker than the paper's statement, without claiming the result fails. No referee report exists. In November Alpöge and Buckmaster claimed the unforced case, recorded separately.
